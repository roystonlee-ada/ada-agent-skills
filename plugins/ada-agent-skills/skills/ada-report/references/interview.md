# Interview: what should the report say?

Use this when the ask does not say who reads the report, for which period, or
what it should cover. Ask in one batch. Ask three questions at most, with
choices, and mark the one you recommend with why. The person is not an Ada
expert, so offer outcomes, not feature names.

## Contents
- Questions
- Report types
- Defaults

## Questions

1. **Who will read it, and what should they decide?** Choices:
   - Our own team, to see where to improve. *(recommended when unsure: it leads to the most useful next step)*
   - A leader or sponsor, to show results and value.
   - A customer or partner of ours, to show how we serve them.
2. **Which period?** Choices: last 30 days *(recommended: enough volume, recent enough to act on)*, last quarter, a month they name, since go-live.
3. **What should it focus on?** Choices:
   - Overall performance and the biggest next win.
   - One topic in depth, such as returns or billing.
   - Where conversations still reach a person, and what could be automated.
   - Customer experience: satisfaction and how customers talk to the agent.

Ask a fourth thing only if the answer would change the report: which channels to
include, or whether a goal or target should appear. Skip it otherwise.

Do not ask for metric names, filters or dates in a format. Work those out from the
period and focus.

## Report types

The focus answer picks the shape. All types keep the same look and the same
rules for transcripts and notes.

| Type | Headline metrics | Charts | Transcripts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance overview | resolution, volume, satisfaction, passed to team | topics by volume, weekly trend | one win, one opportunity |
| Topic in depth | that topic's volume, resolution, satisfaction | how the topic's conversations end | three: resolved, partly, passed on |
| Automation roadmap | share passed to a person, top topics passed on | topics by "passed to team" volume | two that a playbook or action could finish |
| Customer experience | satisfaction, resolution, handle time | satisfaction by topic | one praised, one with a low score |

## Defaults

When the person does not answer, use: audience is their own team, last 30 days,
performance overview. Write the assumption in the report footer so it is visible.
